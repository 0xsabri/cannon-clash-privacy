Privacy Policy – Real Cannon

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Developer: yumeko

Contact: 0xsabrii@gmail.com

The short version

Real Cannon does not collect, send, share or sell any personal data. The game works fully offline.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising identifiers.
  • No ad network in this version (see "Ads" below).
  • No chat and no contact with other players.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, files, location, camera or microphone.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, trophies, coins, gems, cards, boosters, purchases you have made, and settings) is saved locally on your device only. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes it. We cannot restore it, because we never receive it.

Opponents

All opponents in Real Cannon are computer-controlled bots, not real people. No information is exchanged with other players.

In-app purchases

This version of Real Cannon has no in-app purchases. A future version may offer optional purchases (gems, bundles and a Battle Pass premium track). They will be processed by Google Play, under Google's terms and the Google Privacy Policy. We will not receive or store your name, email address or payment details. If a future version sells boosters containing random items, the odds of each item rarity will be shown in the game before purchase.

Ads

This version of Real Cannon contains no ads and no ad network.

A future version may offer one optional rewarded video ad (to multiply the rewards of a match). It will never be shown during your first 24 hours of play, never be forced, and there will be no pop-up (interstitial) ads. Before such a version is released, this policy will be updated to name the ad provider, the data it collects (for example the advertising ID and device information), and your consent choices.

Children

Real Cannon is a cartoon game designed for a general audience and is not directed primarily at children. Because the game collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either, including children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country). Parents can disable in-app purchases on their child's device in the Google Play settings (Settings > Authentication > Require authentication for purchases), or with Google Family Link.

The game engine

Real Cannon is made with Unity. The game includes no Unity Analytics, Unity Ads or Unity Cloud Diagnostics services.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. If you have any question, write to us at the address below. If you live in the EU/EEA or UK, you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Changes

If a future version adds ads, analytics, online features or any other data processing, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the game will ask for consent where the law requires it. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest change.
